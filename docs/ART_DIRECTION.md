# Art direction — Legends of the Arena

## Visual target

A premium 2D fighting game with a painterly-realistic finish. The target is not flat vector art, primitive placeholders, or neon geometry. Every production asset should have deliberate shape language, material definition, lighting, and readable animation silhouettes.

## Character design

- Original fighters with distinct body proportions, costume construction, layered fabrics, metal/leather/stone materials, and recognizable silhouettes.
- Full character sheets: front/side/three-quarter views, expression studies, color keys, and attack/guard/hit pose sheets.
- Animation sets: idle, walk, dash, jump, crouch, light/heavy attacks, block, hit stun, knockdown, victory, and defeat.
- Separate sprite layers where useful (body, costume details, hair, weapon, effects) to support controlled animation and VFX.

## Arena design

- Multi-plane parallax architecture with foreground framing, playable floor, midground structures, and distant skyline.
- Detailed stone, timber, metal, banners, carved ornament, weathering, practical lights, and atmospheric perspective.
- Stage lighting must preserve fighter readability and avoid excessive bloom.

## Combat effects

- Hand-authored impact flashes, directional trails, dust, sparks, and restrained screen shake.
- Strong anticipation, contact, and recovery poses. Hit effects must clarify timing rather than obscure the fighters.

## Asset acceptance checklist

- No primitive-only character or stage art in a release build.
- Consistent perspective, light direction, palette, and line/render treatment.
- Readable silhouettes at the target gameplay camera distance.
- Sprite sheets use documented frame dimensions, pivots, and animation frame rates.
- All third-party assets have recorded licenses and attribution.
